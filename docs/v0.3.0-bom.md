**Wiring caveat:** the v0.2 I2C header (`J2`) is `1=GND, 2=3V3, 3=SDA, 4=SCL`.
Many 4-pin OLED modules are labeled `GND, VCC, SCL, SDA`, so check the
silkscreen and swap SDA/SCL in the harness if needed. Modules usually include
I2C pull-ups; do not leave the PCF8574 backpack connected at the same time.

**Firmware caveat:** the current LCD adapter probes PCF8574 at `0x27`/`0x3F`;
the SSD1309 is at `0x3C`/`0x3D`. Until M20 adds the OLED adapter, bench-test
the panel with a standalone Pico/Arduino sketch rather than the release
firmware.

**Recommendation:** buy 2× HiLetgo 4-pin I2C for immediate M18 bench work (no
rework, direct fit), 1× Waveshare as the 2.42" second source/mechanical
reference, and request volume quotes from DisplayModule and BuyDisplay for rev
A. Use Adafruit only if a known-good reference unit justifies the premium.

## Sourcing and risk notes

- Verify the OLED module's **actual controller** before layout; 2.42" modules
  are sometimes SH1106 or SH1107 despite SSD1309 listings.
- Confirm the female socket accepts the Pico H header length without the Pico
  bottoming out on the PCB.
- The USB mux/ideal-diode parts are lifecycle-sensitive; identify a second
  source before committing to rev A.
- The A-to-A pigtail is a bench-only item. Do not ship it or recommend it to
  users unless D2 explicitly commits to the shared-A design with a safe,
  labeled adapter.
- Record LCSC/JLCPCB basic-vs-extended status when the rev A BOM is fixed.
